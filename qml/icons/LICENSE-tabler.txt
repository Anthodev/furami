Furami chrome icons — attribution and license
=============================================

The outline glyphs shipped in this directory follow the Tabler Icons design
language (24x24 grid, outline strokes, 2 px stroke width, round caps and
joins). They were adapted for Furami's chrome and are bundled as resources so
no icon is ever fetched from a URL or from an OS theme at runtime.

The stroke colour is written as an opaque white so every glyph is a clean
alpha mask: Qt Quick Controls tints buttons through CompositionMode_SourceIn
and the MultiEffect path in BrumeIcon lifts the source to white before
colourising, so the rendered colour is exactly the requested Brume token.

Icons used: camera, headphones, maximize, minimize, refresh, settings,
adjustments, filter, palette, check, x, play, pause, chevron-down, volume,
volume-off.
